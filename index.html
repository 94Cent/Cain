About Us

Founded in 2011, still run by volunteers who show up.

What started as a handful of neighbors sharing a mailing list has grown into a network of over 400 members across the region. We don't have a large office or a large budget — what we have is a habit of connecting the right people at the right moment.

Every member gets a say in what we take on next, and every project is judged by one question: does this make it easier for someone to do good work here?

What We Do

Three ways we help members move work forward.

Introductions

We keep a working map of who is doing what across the region, so a new member is never starting a search from zero.

Shared resources

Meeting space, printing, and a small grants fund are pooled and shared, so smaller groups can punch above their weight.

Monthly gatherings

An open, informal meetup on the first Tuesday of every month — no agenda required, just a reason to be in the same room.
